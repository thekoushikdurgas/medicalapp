4. STATUTORY GST & INVOICING PROMPTS
==============================================================================

[Prompt 4.1 - Statutory GST & Discount Math Engine]
In backend/app/utils/gst.py, ensure all line items and invoice totals strictly comply with Indian GST rules:
- Line Base Amount = Quantity * Rate
- Trade Discounts = Base Amount * (DIS1% / 100) -> Cascading DIS2%
- Taxable Value = Base Amount - Trade Discounts
- State GST (SGST) = Taxable Value * (SGST% / 100) [Default 2.5% for pharmaceuticals]
- Central GST (CGST) = Taxable Value * (CGST% / 100) [Default 2.5% for pharmaceuticals]
- Line Total = Taxable Value + SGST + CGST
- Invoice Grand Total = Sum(Line Totals) with statutory rounding to nearest Rupee.
Learn, understand, and analyze deeply, breaking tasks into smaller steps.

[Prompt 4.2 - Printable Tax Invoice Generator (MAA Eswari Pharmaceuticals)]
In backend/app/utils/invoice_gen.py, generate clean, standard-compliant, printable HTML/PDF tax invoices:
Header:
- Seller: MAA Eswari Pharmaceuticals, Benachity, Durgapur-13, GSTIN: 19BKMPG6092H1ZO, D.L. No: 20B/21B.
- Buyer: Shopkeeper Name, Store Address, GSTIN, Drug License Number.
- Invoice No (INV-YYYYMMDD-XXXX), Date, Order Ref, Place of Supply (West Bengal Code 19).
Table:
- Sl, Product Description, Pack, Batch, Expiry, HSN, Qty, Free Goods, MRP, Rate, DIS1%, Taxable, SGST, CGST, Amount.
Footer:
- Bank Remittance Details (SBI A/C, IFSC), Drug & Cosmetics Act 1940 Statutory Declaration, Authorized Signature.
